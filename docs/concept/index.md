# Hitotoki Collective

## Introduction

The Hitotoki Collective leaves cultural traces — signposts for the forecestors who will navigate their own paths in their own times. Each trace is anchored in a multi-dimensional creative space where the moment itself is quintessential. Painting, music, film, photography, and movement converge in live multimedia performances staged in sacred places such as the ancient temples of Kyoto, where time opens onto a primordial timelessness.

The Hitotoki Collective takes a counter-intuitive approach to the economics of culture. Funded by sponsors in tune with the project's spirit, the analog fruits of each performance are offered freely to the guardians. Each offering is made as a mark of profound and joyful respect — yet it would be remiss not to declare a second motive: the belief that these guardians offer the best assurance that the signposts will withstand the vicissitudes of history.
