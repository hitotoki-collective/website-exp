### Montages

Montages are edited assemblies and cannot be regenerated from a single
timecode. Picture is cropped to drop the burned-in subtitle band; audio is one
unbroken 30s passage. Shot lists are `start:duration` in seconds.

The `-9x16` variants are true full-bleed vertical: the subtitle band is
cropped off the 4K master first, then a 9:16 frame is taken from the clean
region, so no letterboxing. Crops are centred unless a shot needed a
different offset.

MON-01 is the exception throughout: it was supplied separately from a
subtitle-free source, so it is neither cropped nor reproducible from this
master, and its `-9x16` stays letterboxed because that 1080p source is too
low-resolution to crop to vertical without visible softening.

A third field on a shot is the horizontal crop offset used for the vertical
variant, where centring would have missed the subject.

### Subtitles

The master carries burned-in captions and no subtitle stream. `PRF-02-VID-00-SUB-ja.srt`
and `PRF-02-VID-00-SUB-en.srt` were recovered by OCR (Apple Vision) over the caption
band sampled at 2 fps, with cue boundaries inferred from where the text changes.

`PRF-02-VID-00-SUB-ja-en.srt` is an English translation of the Japanese cues, on the
same timings. `PRF-02-VID-00-SUB-en-combined.srt` interleaves it with the native
English cues to give one continuous English track over the whole film; the two
sets do not overlap in time, so no cue was dropped or retimed.

`PRF-02-VID-00-SUB-en-combined.vtt` carries the combined track with speaker
attribution. This film has no interview transcripts, so attribution rests on
on-screen name cards, what each cue says about the other participants, and the
fact that the subtitle language inverts the spoken one. Every cue carries a NOTE
giving its evidence. Every cue is attributed: the passages that evidence in the
film could not settle were confirmed by the producers and are marked as such.
Sherif Shaw and Mark Greenslade speak in the film but are not listed among the
artists above; they appear under Executive Producers.

They are machine-read and unproofed: timings are accurate to about half a second,
and occasional characters are misread. Correct them in place rather than
re-running the extraction.
